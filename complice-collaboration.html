The Idea

The whole website is
one day at the lake.

A private house on a Swiss lake, and a visitor who has been let in on its secret. Scrolling moves the day. Every page opens in the first light of morning and closes at dusk in the footer. Warm stone, still water and real sun, with no pure white and no pure black anywhere.

Each product
in its element.

The shop gives every formula its own place in nature, matched to when and how it is used. Every bottle is the real one, with its true label.

The ingredients,
worn like couture.

Each product page carries one art image built from a real ingredient on its label. Barley frozen in lake ice. Chicory sinking into the deep sea. Coralline seaweed in a tide pool. Balloon vine in the first rain.

Five promises.

The brand's five pillars, each given a single specimen of a real ingredient. One quiet thought per tile.

The house,
for investors.

The same world carries the investor pages. The full range at the water's edge at dawn, built in one light so the glass and gold reflect the real lake.

Born on the water.

Our Story and Sustainability stay on the lake. A glacier at dawn above the waterline and clean stones below it. A single chicory flower floating on clear Swiss water, with nothing to hide.
