The House

Not content. Short films.

Realm of Steez is a short film production house, built for brands and visionaries who understand that the distance between content and cinema is the distance between noise and legacy.

Every project begins with one conviction: your brand deserves more than a video. Precise visual language, deliberate composition, and sequences built to outlast the moment they were made for.

Production Access

Three levels. One standard.

Micro 6–20 s · Short 30–90 s · Long 2–10+ min

Level I · Core

Visual Architecture

  • Concept direction
  • Script development
  • Character creation
  • Environment & set design
  • Wardrobe styling
  • Structured edit

Foundation only. A clean visual master.

Level II · Enhanced

Cinematic Completion

  • Everything in Core
  • Custom-composed soundtrack
  • Sound design
  • Optional voiceover
  • 2K master finish
  • Image refinement

Refined. Immersive. Release-ready.

Level III · Signature

Definitive Release

  • Everything in Enhanced
  • Cinematic colour grade
  • 4K master & audio mastering
  • Trailer edit & cutdowns
  • Multi-format export suite
  • Promotional still frames

Not an edit. A controlled release.
