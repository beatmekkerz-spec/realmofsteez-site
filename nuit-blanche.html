The Idea

A goggle for the hours
after the lifts close.

NUIT BLANCHE is an invented Chamonix optics house, and this is its first launch: a frameless gold-mirror ski goggle that sees in the dark. Not a night-vision gadget. A lens you would wear anyway, that happens to turn midnight into dusk.

The film doesn't explain the technology. It puts you on the mountain at midnight, takes every light away, and lets you feel the moment the dark goes clear.

Always the last one on the mountain.

Midnight.

Clunk.

Inside the Lens

Lines and light.
Nothing to read.

01

Starlight Sensor

A pinpoint on the bridge that sees by the light of the stars alone. Where the eye finds nothing, the lens finds the mountain.

02

Gold Contour Vision

The dark lifts to a clear, cold dusk blue. Every edge, ridge and drop is traced in a thin line of gold, so the terrain arrives before you do.

03

Nothing to Read

No numbers, no menus, no icons. At speed you don't read. You see. The display is lines and light, and nothing else.

04

Frameless Gold Mirror

The whole mountain inside the lens. From the outside, all anyone sees is a glint of gold in the dark.

The dark was never the problem.

Colour Strategy

Black, snow, and one colour of light.

Everything is built to lose light. Deep night blacks, cold snow, floodlight white that dies beat by beat. Gold is the only warm tone allowed in the frame: the lens, the sensor, the contour lines. When everything else goes dark, gold is what's left.
