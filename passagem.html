PASSAGEM

There's a ring in this coffee.
Some things are worth passing on.

The Idea

One bica.
One ring. One city.

Café Passagem is an invented Lisbon coffee house, and this is its first film. A barista slides a bica across the marble with an engagement ring resting on the crema, and the cup sets off across the city, hand to hand, stranger to stranger, every one of them a sip away from swallowing a proposal.

It rides a tram, a tuk-tuk and a freerunner's rooftop sprint before it reaches the one table it was meant for. The ring lands in her palm. He is already on one knee. Behind the counter, the barista simply nods.

one cup.

The Pour

02

The Relay

Hand to hand across Lisbon in hard match-cuts: a conductor, a nun, a tourist, each a sip away from disaster.

03

The Run

Tram, tuk-tuk and rooftop sprint, the city smearing past while the cup stays dead level.

04

The Question

The cup tips, the ring drops into her palm, and she turns to find him on one knee. The barista nods.

Colour Strategy

Tram yellow,
Atlantic gold.

Lisbon in late sun: warm stone, azulejo blue, the black of a perfect crema. The city's tram yellow runs through the whole film, in the trams, in the tuk-tuk, in the silk scarf at her neck, so the eye never loses the thread from the counter to her table.
