The Idea

A car that doesn't
outrun the storm.

PEGASUS is an invented hypercar, and this is its launch film. No spec sheet, no top speed, no driver. Just a myth: a black stallion in a barn on the edge of a salt flat, and a car hidden under a cover beside it, both waiting for the same storm.

The film is built like a legend told in four movements. Fear, then awakening, then the ordeal, then the dawn. The car never fails on screen. When the lightning finally finds it, it doesn't break. It feeds.

The Film

Four movements,
one legend.

01

Fear

A stallion alone in a barn as the storm arrives, calmed only by the glow under a cover beside him.

02

Awakening

Wings unfold, the cover falls, the doors blow open. Horse and car leave the barn as one.

03

The Ordeal

A gauntlet of strikes across the salt, a flight through the storm's heart, and the one bolt meant for them.

04

Home over a mirror of rain. The storm spent, the legend born, the sun cresting behind them.

Colour Strategy

Bruised violet,
then gold.

The storm owns the palette: lilac and bruised violet over white salt, with the car's six blades of light as the only clean white in the frame. Every strike pushes the violet harder until the breach, where the whole film turns to dawn gold and the rain becomes a mirror.
