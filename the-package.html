The Idea

A spy heist with
a second story inside.

An old craftsman asks his phone what a buyer should get, and the answer lists everyone but him. So he hands a plain brown parcel to an agent, and the agent takes it into the tower every buyer asks: past the scanners, through the floor of rivals, under the lasers, into the vault, all the way to the one top slot.

On the surface it is a heist. Underneath, every obstacle is a real reason AI shopping assistants pass a product over, and every way through is the fix. The parcel never opens, so whoever is watching sees their own product inside it.
