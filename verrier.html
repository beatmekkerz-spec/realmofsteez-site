VERRIER

Two watches. One minute.
Time, kept for two.

The Idea

A moonphase made
for two.

VERRIER is an invented Swiss watch house, and this is its first pair: two moonphase watches, one with the moon in its window and one with the Earth. A father wears his on a night train out of Locarno. His daughter wears hers on the moon.

The film never names a complication. It stops time between them, then lets a single minute travel three hundred and eighty thousand kilometres, from his crown to her wrist.

Colour Strategy

Two worlds, one warm metal.

His world is lamplight and timber on a winter night; hers is hard sun on grey dust and black sky. The only thing the two share is the watch: midnight blue and gold, the same in both. When the screen splits, it is the one thing that matches.
